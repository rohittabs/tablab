TabLab

Write it. Hear it. Play along.

A free tab editor for guitar and ukulele students.

Nothing to install and no account. It runs in your browser.

Swipe up Scroll or press ↓

Two ways to read

Notation on top, tab below

Press Shift N, or the Notation button, to show standard notation above the tab. Press it again for the tab alone.

Sharps and flats follow the key signature you set for the song.

Guitar and ukulele

Any tuning, any key

Guitar
Standard, drop D, open G, DADGAD, or your own
Ukulele
High G, low G, D tuning, baritone, or your own

Set a capo up to fret 12 and the key signature. Add chord names, strum arrows, and labels like Verse and Chorus.

Playback

Hear what you wrote

Press Space. TabLab plays the tab with a plucked string sound, and the playhead shows where you are.

You also hear each note as you type it, so a wrong fret stands out straight away.

Practice

Slow it down. Loop the hard part.

60%Same pitch, just slower
25%50%75%100%125%150%

Set any speed from 25% to 150%. Press L to loop the bar you are on, or the beats you selected.

Keeping time

1234

Metronome on or off

The count shows up big on the screen, so you can read it from your chair with the guitar in your hands.

Backing track

Play along with the real song

Load an MP3 or another audio file, then press Auto sync. TabLab finds the tempo and the first beat for you.

The tab and the recording play together, at full speed or slowed down, and the recording keeps its pitch.

Real bands drift

Drag a bar onto its beat

If the band speeds up or slows down, drag that bar's line onto its beat in the waveform. TabLab pins it there and stretches the bars in between to follow.
